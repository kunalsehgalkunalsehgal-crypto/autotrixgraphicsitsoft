QUICK START
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html. It already contains Steps 1-5 including original header, carousel and trust.
3. Keep assets/ next to index.html, and keep style.css and script.js in same folder.

ADD TO YOUR EXISTING STEP 3 PROJECT WITHOUT REPLACING FILES
1. Copy services.html into index.html right AFTER the trust section and before </main>.
2. Copy benefits.html directly AFTER services.html and before </main>.
3. Append services.css, benefits.css and animations.css IN THAT ORDER to style.css.
4. Append animations.js once at the END of script.js.
5. Keep your existing assets folder (photos are reused from step 3).
